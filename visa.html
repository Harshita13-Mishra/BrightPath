VISA PROCESS

Student Visa Process

Follow these important steps when preparing for your student visa application.

01

University Admission

Receive an admission offer from your selected university or institution.

02

Prepare Documents

Organize your academic, financial and other required supporting documents.

03

Complete Visa Application

Fill out the appropriate student visa application and submit the required details.

04

Visa Interview

Prepare for an interview or biometric appointment if required.

05

Visa Decision

Track your application and wait for the official visa decision.

06

Prepare for Departure

After approval, prepare your travel and accommodation arrangements.

Commonly Required Documents

Passport

🎓

Academic Documents

💰

Financial Documents

Proof of funds or financial support as required.

🌐

Language Test

IELTS, PTE or other accepted language test results.

✍️

SOP / Statement

Statement of purpose or other supporting statement when required.

📑

Visa Forms

Completed visa forms and supporting application documents.
